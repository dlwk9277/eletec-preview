About Eletec关于 Eletec

A Singapore lift contractor since 2004.自2004年起的新加坡电梯承包商。

Lifts for homes and buildings.为住宅和建筑而设的电梯。

Eletec Elevators Singapore Pte Ltd has been a lift contractor since 2004. We install home lifts, glass and circular lifts, passenger lifts and cargo lifts, with custom finishing.Eletec Elevators Singapore Pte Ltd 自2004年起从事电梯承包。我们安装家用电梯、玻璃及圆形电梯、客梯和货梯，并提供定制装饰。

For lifts already in place, we provide regular maintenance, upgrading of old lifts and emergency response.对于已有的电梯，我们提供定期保养、旧电梯翻新和紧急响应。
