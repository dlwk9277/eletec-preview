About Eletec关于 Eletec

A Singapore lift company since 2004, known for premium home lifts.自2004年起扎根新加坡的电梯公司，以高端家用电梯见长。

Lifts made to fit the building.按建筑量身打造的电梯。

Established in 2004, ELETEC Elevators Singapore Pte Ltd has been a pioneer in the supply and installation of premium home lifts. We combine cost leadership with product and service differentiation: well-priced lifts, made to order and properly looked after.ELETEC Elevators Singapore Pte Ltd 成立于2004年，是高端家用电梯供应与安装的先行者。我们兼顾成本优势与产品、服务的差异化：价格合理、按需定制，并有完善的售后保养。

We work closely with designers and architects on bespoke lifts, and we source through ODM and OEM partners in China, Taiwan and Europe, including Fermator doors from Spain.我们与设计师和建筑师紧密合作定制电梯，并通过中国、台湾和欧洲的 ODM 与 OEM 合作伙伴采购，包括来自西班牙的 Fermator 电梯门。
