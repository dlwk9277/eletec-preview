Your lift, kept safe and legal.让您的电梯安全又合规。

Lifts in Singapore need regular servicing and a yearly test before their Permit to Operate is renewed. As a BCA-registered lift maintenance contractor (RW02B), we handle both, for lifts we installed and for other brands.在新加坡，电梯需要定期保养，并在每年更新运行许可证前通过检测。作为 BCA 注册电梯维修保养承包商（RW02B），这两项我们都能处理，无论是我们安装的电梯还是其他品牌。

Regular inspections, preventive maintenance and timely servicing catch problems early, so the lift stays reliable and safe.定期检查、预防性维护和及时保养，能及早发现问题，让电梯可靠又安全。
